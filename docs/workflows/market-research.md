# Market research

Use this template to investigate a specific market and audience, rank evidenced
problems and propose three concepts. It is an editable instruction set for the
shared SDK runtime, not a team of independently deployed research agents.

Supply `market` and `audience`. Optional `competitorsHint` gives known alternatives;
`handover` describes the reader and desired delivery format. The workflow does not
send the resulting document anywhere.

The four steps research five perspectives with live sources, rank problems,
propose concepts, and independently review the documents. The perspectives are
creative friction, everyday struggle, abandoned workarounds, emerging behavior
and underserved moments. They are lenses within one research conversation.

Instructions require real complaints, short quotes, source URLs and displayed
metadata; unknown dates and community sizes stay unknown. Ranking uses reach,
evidence strength, emotional weight and how unsolved the problem remains. Concept
suggestions preserve those evidence limits and remain unvalidated hypotheses.
The separate reviewer checks sources and the declared criteria using live search.

Prepare this template through the [shared workflow editor](README.md#create-review-and-run).
No process registration or promotion is required. Source-backed research and a
passing model review do not establish market demand, product quality or revenue.
